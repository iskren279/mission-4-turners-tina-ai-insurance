# Level 5 ADV Mission 4 – Tina Insurance Recommendation Assistant

## Introduction

Tina is an AI-powered insurance recommendation assistant built for Mission 4.

Tina helps users choose a suitable car insurance product by asking questions about their vehicle and insurance needs. Based on the user's answers, Tina recommends one or more suitable insurance products.

The available insurance products are:

- Mechanical Breakdown Insurance (MBI)
- Comprehensive Car Insurance
- Third Party Car Insurance

Tina follows the business rules provided for the insurance products. For example, Comprehensive Car Insurance is only available for vehicles less than 10 years old, and MBI is not available for trucks or racing cars.
